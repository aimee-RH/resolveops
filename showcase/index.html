Ground decisions in evidence

TRUST BOUNDARIES
01Scope comes from the server

The model cannot choose the tenant, principal, or business resource. Tools enforce the run's permissions and budget again.

02Drafts are not evidence

The final model call sees persisted snapshots only. Its cited IDs are constrained and checked again by the backend.

03The model cannot repair

Policy selects the outcome. A versioned proposal, separate approver, and human execution gate any local subscription write.

The API authorization path uses the same workbench and evidence lifecycle with its own read-only tools. The four tools shown here illustrate the subscription path.
